You can bookmark individual manga/novel titles for easy access. This allows you to jump directly to a specific manga/novel series and start reading immediately. 

Here’s how to bookmark a manga/novel:

## Steps:

::steps{level=5}
   ##### Open a Manga/Novel Site:
   - First, make sure you have a manga/novel site added to your manga/novel site widget. If not, follow the [steps to add a manga/novel site](/getting-started/how-to/add-manga-site).
   - Click the manga/novel site from your widget to open it.

![Screenshot of open manga site from widget](/add-manga/step1.webp)  

   ##### Navigate to the Manga/Novel You Want to Bookmark:
   - Browse the manga/novel catalog and find the manga/novel series you want to bookmark.
   - Open the manga/novel's detail page.

   ##### Run Add-ons for Manga/Novel Details:
   - If you're not already in manga/novel reading mode, a prompt will appear asking if you want to run the manga/novel detail add-ons.
   - Click **Run Add-ons** to activate the enhanced features for the manga/novel.

![Screenshot of "Run Add-ons" prompt](/add-manga/step2.webp)  

   ##### Bookmark the Manga:
   - Tap the three-dotted menu button at the top right of your browser.
   - Select **Bookmark** to save the manga/novel series.
   - The manga/novel will now appear in the manga/novel bookmark widget on your home page.

![Screenshot of bookmarking the manga site](/add-manga/step3.webp)   
::


Now, you can easily access your bookmarked manga from the widget and continue reading!

![Screenshot of the manga widget](/add-manga/step4.webp)
