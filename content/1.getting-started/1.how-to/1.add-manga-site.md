Adding a manga/novel site to your widget will allow you to quickly access your favorite manga/novel websites with enhanced features for a better reading experience.

Follow the steps below to add a manga/novel site:

## Steps:

::steps{level=5}
   ##### Visit a Manga/Novel Site:
   - Open the app and search for a manga/novel site, or click the "Add Manga Site" button on the manga site widget, or click the "Add Novel Site" button on the novel site widget.
   - Choose a manga/novel site from the search results and visit it.

![Screenshot of manga site search result](/add-manga-site/step1.webp)

   ##### Run Add-ons Prompt:
   - Once you're on a supported manga/novel site, a prompt will appear asking if you want to run the manga/novel catalog add-ons.
   - Click **Run Add-ons** to activate the enhanced reading features for the site.

![Screenshot of "Run Add-ons" prompt](/add-manga-site/step2.webp)

   ##### Bookmark the Manga/Novel Site:
   - After running the add-ons, tap the three-dotted menu button at the top right of your browser.
   - Select **Bookmark** from the menu to save the manga/novel site.
   - The site will now appear in the manga/novel site widget on your home page for easy access in the future.

![Screenshot of bookmarking the manga site](/add-manga-site/step3.webp)
::

Now you can quickly visit your favorite manga/novel site by clicking it from the manga/novel site widget!

![Screenshot of the manga site widget](/add-manga-site/step4.webp)
